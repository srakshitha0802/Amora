## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: What is the minimum order quantity for corporate orders?
> All corporate merchandise items (bottles, mugs, desk mats, and mousepads) require a minimum order quantity (MOQ) of **36 pieces**. This enables us to maintain strict quality standards, precise colour calibration, and competitive wholesale pricing.

### Q2: Can I order just 1 personal keepsake or polaroid pack?
> Yes, absolutely! Amora Creative items — including polaroid packs, stickers, bookmarks, photostrips, and posters — have **No MOQ**. You can order as few as 1 pack or item.

### Q3: How do I place an order or get a quote?
> Amora does not use an impersonal checkout cart. Instead, browse our catalogue, add your favourite items to your **Quote Tray**, and click **"Submit on WhatsApp"**. This connects you directly with our Kalyan studio team to discuss custom text, photos, and delivery schedules.

### Q4: Are shipping charges included in the listed prices?
> Listed prices for corporate merchandise exclude shipping charges. Shipping is calculated based on total package weight, box dimensions, and destination pincode. For retail personal orders, standard shipping rates apply at confirmation.

### Q5: How long does production and delivery take?
> Personal keepsakes typically dispatch within 2–4 business days. Corporate bulk orders (36+ units) are produced and dispatched within 7–12 business days following artwork approval. Express pan-India courier shipping takes 2–5 days depending on location.
